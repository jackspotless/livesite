House Cleaning in Grayhawk

North Scottsdale's master plan just above the 101 — The Park and The Retreat, two desert golf courses, and a mix of condos, townhomes, family homes, and custom estates.

Cleaning Grayhawk

Grayhawk is a roughly 1,600-acre master-planned community in North Scottsdale, just north of the Loop 101 between Scottsdale Road and Pima Road. It opened in 1996 and finished building in 2008, so most homes are 20 to 30 years old. About half of its homes are single-family and half are townhomes and condos, which gives it more variety than most North Scottsdale communities.

Grayhawk is ZIP 85255. We cover both sides of the community.

The Grayhawk homes we clean

  • The Park — The non-gated western side, mostly single-family production homes with greenbelts and parks between neighborhoods.
  • The Retreat — The gated eastern side, with a mix of custom homes, production homes, and townhomes, many with golf course views.
  • Condos and townhomes — Attached homes throughout the community, popular with part-time residents.
  • Golf course homes — Homes along the Talon and Raptor courses at Grayhawk Golf Club.

What's particular about cleaning a Grayhawk home

Desert landscaping and fine dust

Grayhawk is high Sonoran Desert, with natural washes and open desert between neighborhoods. Fine dust works its way in through doors and window tracks, and it shows on dark floors and furniture fast. Regular cleaning keeps it from building up into a gritty layer.

Homes reaching their second or third remodel

At 20 to 30 years old, many Grayhawk homes have had kitchens or baths redone while keeping original flooring or cabinets. We clean each surface with the right products.

Townhomes mean stairs

About half of Grayhawk's homes are townhomes and condos, and many put the living space or bedrooms up a flight of stairs. Multi-level homes take longer than single-level homes of the same size, because every floor gets its own trip with supplies. The instant quote prices by size and bathrooms, so the number you see reflects the real work.

Gated access in The Retreat

For homes inside The Retreat's gates, add access instructions to your booking notes once and we'll handle them every visit.

Part-time residents

Grayhawk's condos and villas draw a lot of winter residents. Pre-arrival deep cleans in the fall and closing cleans in the spring are common.

Greenbelts and family homes in The Park

The Park was designed around greenbelts, parks, and play areas, with Grayhawk's own elementary school in the middle of it. Grass, sand, and whatever the kids bring home from the park end up in entries, kids' bathrooms, and the family room. We put the extra time where the traffic actually is.

Golf weeks and winter guests

The Talon and Raptor courses, plus the TPC Scottsdale a few minutes south, make Grayhawk a popular place to host visitors in winter, especially around the WM Phoenix Open in February. Guest-room refreshes and pre-arrival cleans fill up fast from January through March. Book a week or two ahead if you have people coming.

How often Grayhawk homeowners book cleaning

Families in The Park mostly book bi-weekly. Larger homes in The Retreat often go weekly or bi-weekly. Condo and townhome owners tend toward bi-weekly or monthly while they're in town.
