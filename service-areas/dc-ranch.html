House Cleaning in DC Ranch

North Scottsdale's master plan at the foot of the McDowell Mountains — family neighborhoods near Market Street, the Country Club, and the custom estates of Silverleaf.

Cleaning DC Ranch

DC Ranch is a large master-planned community in North Scottsdale, north of the Loop 101 and running up into the McDowell Mountains along the edge of the McDowell Sonoran Preserve. It's built around two community centers, Desert Camp and the Homestead, plus Market Street and DC Ranch Crossing for everyday errands. Its neighborhoods range from villas and family homes to some of the largest custom estates in the Valley.

DC Ranch is ZIP 85255. We cover the whole community, Silverleaf included.

The DC Ranch homes we clean

  • Village neighborhoods — Single-family homes and townhomes on walkable streets near Market Street and the community parks.
  • The Country Club at DC Ranch — A gated golf neighborhood with larger homes and custom builds.
  • Silverleaf — A separate guard-gated enclave of custom and semi-custom homes in the McDowell canyons, many 5,000 square feet and up.
  • Villas and condos — Attached homes near Market Street, popular with part-time residents.

What's particular about cleaning a DC Ranch home

Large homes need real time

Many DC Ranch and Silverleaf homes are far bigger than a typical Valley house, with more bathrooms, guest suites, and casitas. Our instant quote prices by size and bathrooms, so the work gets the time it actually takes instead of being rushed to hit a flat number.

Natural stone, wood, and specialty finishes

Custom homes here often use limestone, marble, reclaimed wood, plaster, and other finishes that don't tolerate harsh cleaners. We use pH-neutral products on stone and gentle methods on specialty surfaces. If your builder or designer recommended specific products, leave them out and we'll use them.

Mountain-edge dust and big glass

Homes along the preserve get desert dust and wind off the mountains, and they tend to have big view windows. We clean interior glass and accessible door glass every visit. Exterior and high windows are a specialty trade, and we'll tell you that up front.

Guard gates and household staff

Silverleaf and the Country Club are gated, and many larger homes have house managers or other staff. Add access and coordination details to your booking once and we'll follow them every visit.

Hosting around Market Street

DC Ranch has a busy community calendar, from the farmers market and holiday events at Market Street to gatherings at Desert Camp and the Homestead. If you're hosting before or after an event, a pre-party clean and a post-party reset are easy to schedule. Tell us what's happening and we'll focus the time on the kitchen, entertaining spaces, and guest bathrooms.

Living on the preserve

Many DC Ranch and Silverleaf homes back right onto the McDowell Sonoran Preserve. The views are the point, but the desert also brings fine dust on patios and ledges, cobwebs in corners and garages, and the occasional scorpion. We clear webs and debris every visit. Pest treatment is a separate service, and we'll say so rather than pretend otherwise.

How often DC Ranch homeowners book cleaning

Weekly is common in the larger homes, especially in Silverleaf and the Country Club. Families in the village neighborhoods mostly book bi-weekly. Part-time residents usually book a pre-arrival deep clean and a closing clean around their season.

Yes. We use pH-neutral cleaners on natural stone and gentle methods on plaster, wood, and other specialty surfaces. If you have recommended products, leave them out and we'll use them.
