House Cleaning in Troon

High desert around Pinnacle Peak and Troon Mountain — custom homes tucked among the boulders, golf course homes, and some of the best views in the Valley.

Cleaning Troon and Troon North

Troon is the high-desert corner of North Scottsdale around Pinnacle Peak. Troon Village sits southeast of the peak around Troon Mountain and the Troon Country Club. Troon North, about 1,800 acres, wraps the northern foothills of Pinnacle Peak between Pima Road and Alma School Parkway, around the Monument and Pinnacle courses. Both are known for granite boulder outcroppings, saguaros, and homes designed to disappear into the landscape.

Troon Village is mostly ZIP 85255, and Troon North is mostly 85262. We cover both, along with the surrounding Pinnacle Peak neighborhoods.

The Troon homes we clean

  • Custom desert contemporaries — Large homes built into the terrain, often with walls of glass facing the peak or the city lights.
  • Golf course homes — Homes along the Troon North and Troon Country Club courses.
  • Gated enclaves — Communities like Candlewood Estates and Pinnacle Canyon, each with its own gate and access rules.
  • Patio homes and townhomes — Smaller lock-and-leave homes, popular with part-time residents.

What's particular about cleaning a Troon home

Real desert means real dust

Homes here sit inside natural desert rather than next to it. Fine dust blows in through doors and window tracks, and it builds on patios, ledges, and dark floors quickly. Regular cleaning keeps it from getting ground into floors and furniture.

Glass everywhere

Troon homes are built for the view, with floor-to-ceiling windows, pocketing doors, and glass railings. Hard water and dust both show on it. We clean interior glass and accessible door glass every visit. Exterior and high windows are a specialty trade.

Natural materials inside

Flagstone, travertine, exposed beams, and stone fireplaces are common. We use pH-neutral products on stone and dust beams and ledges carefully rather than soaking anything.

Critters come with the territory

Living in the desert means occasional uninvited guests: spiders, scorpions, and the cobwebs and debris that come with them in garages, corners, and patio doors. We clear webs and debris as part of every clean. Pest control itself is a separate service.

Pinnacle Peak in the backyard

Pinnacle Peak Park's trail starts right between Troon's neighborhoods, and plenty of residents hike it before breakfast. Between the trail, the golf, and desert all around, dust and grit come in on shoes more than people expect. Entry floors, mudrooms, and the laundry room take the brunt, and we clean them accordingly.

Big homes, big patios

Troon homes are built for outdoor living: covered patios, outdoor kitchens, and pocketing doors that open the great room to the desert. Indoors, the rooms next to those doors pick up the most dust and grit. We give them extra time each visit, and we clean accessible door glass and tracks as part of the work.

How often Troon homeowners book cleaning

Full-time residents in larger homes often book weekly or bi-weekly. Many Troon homes are seasonal, so pre-arrival deep cleans in the fall and closing cleans in the spring are especially common here.
