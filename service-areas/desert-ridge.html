House Cleaning in Desert Ridge

North Phoenix's big master plan along the 101 — Aviano, Fireside, Sky Crossing, Talinn, and thousands of family homes built from the late '90s to today.

Cleaning Desert Ridge

Desert Ridge is a master-planned community in north Phoenix covering about 5,700 acres along the Loop 101, just west of the Scottsdale line. The first homes sold in 1996, the Mayo Clinic Hospital opened in 1998, and building has continued in phases ever since. Today it's anchored by Desert Ridge Marketplace and the JW Marriott resort, with neighborhoods for every stage of life around them.

Desert Ridge is mostly ZIPs 85050 and 85054. We cover the whole master plan.

The Desert Ridge neighborhoods we clean

  • Aviano — One of the original master subdivisions, with single-family homes, condos and townhomes at the Villages at Aviano, and resort-style community amenities.
  • Fireside — A large, family-oriented development with multiple builder series, from condos to single-family homes.
  • Sky Crossing — Newer neighborhoods on the north side, with recent construction and newer finishes.
  • Talinn — A gated community of single-level and two-story homes.
  • Condos and townhomes near the Marketplace — Attached homes and apartments close to shopping and the 101.

What's particular about cleaning a Desert Ridge home

Two-story family homes are the norm

A lot of Desert Ridge is 4-bedroom, 2.5- or 3-bath production homes on two floors. Those take longer than a single-story home of the same size because of the stairs and extra bathrooms. Our instant quote prices by size and bathrooms, so you see the real number up front.

Newer neighborhoods still have construction dust

In the parts of Desert Ridge that are still building out, fine construction dust drifts in through doors and settles on sills, blinds, and ceiling fans. A deep clean to start, then a recurring schedule, keeps it under control.

Kids, pets, and pools

Desert Ridge is full of busy households. High-traffic kitchens, kids' bathrooms, and floors near the back door get dirty faster, and that's where we focus extra time.

Monsoon dust

Open desert still borders parts of the community. From July through September, dust storms can coat patios, window tracks, and anything near a door in one afternoon.

A community that keeps growing

Desert Ridge was planned for about 15,000 homes, and it's been filling in for nearly 30 years. That means the original late-'90s Aviano and Fireside homes are on their second round of updates, while the newest sections still have the glossy finishes and construction residue of a new build. We clean older and newer surfaces with the right products for each.

Community pools and kids' gear

Desert Ridge neighborhoods are built around community pools, splash pads, and parks. That means wet towels, sunscreen on door handles, and sand from playgrounds in entryways and kids' bathrooms. We plan extra time for bathrooms and entries in households with young kids.

How often Desert Ridge homeowners book cleaning

Bi-weekly is by far the most common schedule for Desert Ridge families. Larger households and homes with several pets often go weekly. Condo and townhome owners tend toward bi-weekly or monthly.
